The finding

Two candidate signals, measured on the same 328,186 organisations that filed a long-form return in fiscal 2018 or 2019, scored on whether they were still filing by fiscal 2022. Both tables hold within every one of ten spending deciles, so neither result is a story about big charities.

How it works

Built for the DEV Weekend Challenge: Generosity Edition, 4 to 7 September 2026. Data: IRS Statistics of Income annual extracts of Form 990 and Form 990-EZ financial data plus the Exempt Organizations Business Master File. Both are works of the US federal government and in the public domain.

Keepalive is not a charity rating and not financial advice. Every number on this site comes from an organisation's own filing, is labelled with the fiscal year it came from and can be checked against the source.
